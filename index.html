02 — SECURITY RESEARCH

UNSW-NB15 · intrusion detection

Research into vertical federated learning for network attack detection, including model evaluation and missing-client experiments.

Research case study

Compared SVM, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, MLP, and Neural VFL across shared and heterogeneous representations with zero masking and mean imputation. Partitioned 47 features into five semantic clients and compared VFL results with centralized baselines. Evaluation uses precision, recall, F1, and confusion matrices.

Reported outcome: the best Neural VFL run reached 97.62% Attack F1; centralized XGBoost reached 98.10%. Small differences are not evidence of general superiority because neural and autoencoder runs were not globally seeded.

03 — DISTRIBUTED LEARNING

ToN-IoT · learning with missing clients

Investigating how distributed learning behaves when device data is unavailable, with prototype-based F-ETL approaches.

Research focus

Aligned five IoT device streams by timestamp using an outer join to preserve naturally missing clients. Compared autoencoder-based classifiers and end-to-end Neural VFL with zero masking and mean imputation. Shared-AE LightGBM with zero masking achieved 75.51% Attack F1 in the reported experiments. Existing centralized results use a different test population and cannot support a direct performance comparison. Prototype-based F-ETL and malicious-client robustness remain further research directions.
